Træning er kun en del af regnestykket Training is only part of the equation

Du kan træne perfekt fire dage om ugen og stadig stå stille. Det er ikke fordi du er doven — det er fordi de andre to faktorer ikke er på plads. You can train perfectly four days a week and still go nowhere. Not because you're lazy — because the other two factors aren't in place.

~20%

Træning Training

Stimulus til muskel- og styrke-vækst. Vigtig — men begrænset effekt uden de næste to. The stimulus for muscle and strength growth. Important — but limited effect without the next two.

~50%

Kost Nutrition

Energi til at træne, protein til at bygge muskel, kalorie-balance til at tabe fedt. Uden de rigtige tal i den rigtige retning sker der intet. Energy to train, protein to build muscle, calorie balance to lose fat. Without the right numbers in the right direction, nothing happens.

~30%

Søvn & restitution Sleep & recovery

Det er her kroppen reparerer og adapterer. På diæt med kun 5,5 timers søvn taber du tre gange så meget muskel og halvt så meget fedt som ved 8,5 timer (Nedeltcheva 2010). This is when the body actually repairs and adapts. On a diet with only 5.5 hours of sleep, you lose 3× more muscle and half as much fat as on 8.5 hours (Nedeltcheva 2010).

Vægtning og prioritering bygger på Helms et al. (The Muscle & Strength Pyramid, 2018) og Schoenfeld et al. That's why Training log tracks all three — training, macros AND sleep — in one app. Sleep is logged per night with quality rating and an age-based target band (NSF, Hirshkowitz 2015). Weighting and priorities based on Helms et al. (The Muscle & Strength Pyramid, 2018) and Schoenfeld et al.
